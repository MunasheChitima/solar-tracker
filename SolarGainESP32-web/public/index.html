📚 How to Use This Tool

1

Enter Your Location

Type your address in the field below and select from the suggestions. This helps us get accurate weather and sun position data for your area.

2

Configure Your Solar Setup

Tell us about your solar panels: how many you have, their power rating, and which direction they face. Don't worry if you're not sure—you can use the default values to start.

3

Click "Compute" Button

Once you've entered your information, click the big "Compute" button below. The tool will calculate how much solar energy you can expect for the day.

4

View Your Results

Scroll down to see your forecast! You'll get hourly predictions, battery charge estimates, and a 7-day outlook. You can also export the data or share your results.

Which Direction Do Your Panels Face? 🧭
Tip: In Zimbabwe and other southern hemisphere countries, choose North (N) for maximum sun. In northern countries, choose South (S).
